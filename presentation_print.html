Section 1

1. MOTIVATION

Ecological & Economic Urgency

35%+ Global Solid Waste Volume

Construction and Demolition (C&D) activities generate over 3 billion metric tons of debris every year, outstripping all municipal solid waste streams combined and overwhelming landfill reserves globally.

Ecological Depletion of Aggregates

Rampant river sand dredging and virgin limestone quarrying for concrete production cause catastrophic riverbed erosion, aquatic ecosystem collapse, and heavy Scope-3 embodied carbon emissions.

Substantial Lost Economic Value

Reusable structural steel rebar, dimensional timber, clay bricks, and crushable concrete rubble are routinely discarded together into uncontrolled landfills rather than being segregated and monetized.

The Opportunity for AI Vision & GIS

By deploying edge vision AI coupled with satellite GPS logistics, site engineers can instantly audit, certify, and monetize salvaged debris directly to nearby procurement yards within a circular economic loop.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Core Gaps in Waste Management

"Current construction and demolition waste management practices rely almost exclusively on unverified manual visual inspection, leading to severe material contamination, audit fraud, unrecorded illegal dumping, and fragmented local salvage supply chains."

01

No Auto Classifier

Site supervisors lack real-time computer vision to distinguish clean concrete rubble, clay masonry, and scrap rebar from non-structural debris.

02

Audit Fraud & Spoofing

Existing logging forms accept arbitrary photo uploads (notebook pages, receipts, stock photos) without computerized fracture verification.

03

Fractured Supply Chain

Demolition contractors have excess salvageable rubble but zero direct digital connection to nearby builders or aggregate crushers.

04

Zero ESG Telemetry

Zero automated computation of embodied carbon (CO2e) offsets, municipal landfill airspace conserved, or tipping fee savings.

3. GOALS AND OBJECTIVES

System Objectives
Primary Project Goal
To build a production-grade, AI-driven circular intelligence platform that automates on-site CDW recognition, enforces fraud-proof site audits via GPS reverse-geocoding, and enables peer-to-peer secondary material trading between contractors and buyers.
1

Industrial Vision Classifier

Train deep CNNs (ResNet-34) to categorize 8+ CDW materials (Concrete, Brick, Metal, Timber, Ceramic, Asphalt) with >90% validation accuracy.

2

Anti-Spoofing Filter

Formulate a 2-stage tensor luminance & stroke-density gate to reject non-debris uploads (notebooks, certificates, white paper).

3

Live Satellite GPS Lock

Capture browser GNSS hardware fixes and auto-resolve human-readable site survey addresses via reverse geocoding REST APIs.

4

Geospatial B2B Market

Develop Haversine distance-optimized material discovery, interactive Leaflet mapping, and buyer order transaction management.

5

Live ESG Impact Telemetry

Dynamically compute real-time embodied carbon (0.428 kg CO2e/kg), landfill space saved (1,250 kg/m³), and tipping fee savings.

6

Zero-Mock Architecture

Ensure all dashboard charts, logs, inventory tallies, and order metrics are 100% dynamically sourced from disk-persisted database records.

4. ASSUMPTIONS AND RISKS

Vulnerability & Mitigation Analysis
Category Key Assumption Identified Risk / Vulnerability Mitigation Strategy
Data Quality & Lighting Site photos captured via smartphone cameras in outdoor daylight or site worklights. Extreme dust, motion blur, direct specular sun glares on steel rebar. Color space normalization, adaptive CLAHE contrast enhancement, multi-scale cropping.
Material Contamination Demolition waste may contain mixed aggregates (e.g., concrete slab with rebar). Secondary misclassification or composite material ambiguity. Multi-label secondary prediction ranking and contractor human-in-the-loop override logging.
Network Connectivity Demolition sites have intermittent 4G/5G mobile bandwidth. High latency in server-side AI inference or map tile loading. Client-side canvas tensor preprocessing and local disk-backed persistence caching.
Regulatory Integrity Contractors comply with municipal C&D waste disposal bylaws. Attempted fraud through submission of arbitrary paper receipts or stock images. Anti-spoofing document rejection algorithms, mandatory satellite GPS lock, and audit trails.

9. ALGORITHMS USED

Mathematical Models

1. Anti-Spoofing Document Filter

Integrity Heuristic

Evaluates luminosity and stroke density to reject non-debris documents (notebooks, certificates, invoices):

Paper_Ratio = Σ[L_i > 165 ∧ S_i < 30] / N_pixels
Stroke_Ratio = Σ[L_i < 120] / N_pixels
REJECT IF (Paper > 0.65 ∧ 0.003 ≤ Stroke ≤ 0.09 ∧ σ < 40)

2. ResNet-34 Material Classifier

Deep Learning

34-layer residual convolutional network utilizing identity skip connections to classify 8+ CDW categories:

y = F(x, {W_i}) + x   [Identity Skip Connection]
Head: AdaptiveAvgPool → Linear(512, 256) → Softmax
P(Class_k) = exp(z_k) / Σ exp(z_j)

3. Haversine Logistics Distance

Geospatial

Calculates great-circle spherical transit distance between demolition sites and buyer aggregate yards:

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ1)·cos(φ2)·sin²(Δλ/2)
d = 2R · atan2(√a, √(1-a))   [R = 6,371 km]

4. Embodied Carbon & Landfill LCA

Environmental LCA

Real-time mathematical conversion of diverted kilogram tonnage into verified ESG environmental offsets:

CO2e_Avoided (kg) = Diverted (kg) × 0.428 kg CO2e/kg
Landfill_Airspace (m³) = Diverted (kg) / 1,250 kg/m³
Tipping_Saved (₹) = (Diverted / 1,000) × ₹1,800/ton

const a = Math.sin(dLat/2)**2 + 
  Math.cos(lat1) * Math.cos(lat2) * 
  Math.sin(dLon/2)**2;
const d = 2 * 6371 * 
  Math.atan2(Math.sqrt(a), Math.sqrt(1-a));
return +d.toFixed(1); // km
