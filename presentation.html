Section 1

Project Motivation

35%+ Global Solid Waste Volume

The global construction sector generates over 3 billion metric tons of demolition debris every year, outstripping all municipal solid waste categories combined.

Virgin Resource Depletion

Rampant river sand dredging and virgin limestone quarrying for concrete production cause catastrophic riverbed erosion, ground-water collapse, and heavy Scope-3 carbon emissions.

Lost Economic Value

Reusable structural steel, clean dimensional timber, uncrushed masonry, and recyclable concrete aggregate are routinely discarded into uncontrolled landfills due to absence of rapid grading tools.

The Opportunity for AI & GIS

By deploying edge vision AI coupled with satellite GPS logistics, contractors can instantly audit, certify, and monetize salvaged debris directly to nearby procurement yards within a circular economic loop.

Section 2

Problem Statement

"Current construction and demolition waste (CDW) management practices rely almost entirely on unverified, manual visual inspections. This results in severe material contamination, fraudulent site audit submissions, unrecorded illegal dumping, and fractured local salvage supply chains."

01

No On-Site Auto Classifier

Site supervisors lack real-time computer vision to distinguish clean concrete, scrap rebar, and gypsum drywall.

02

Audit Fraud & Paper Spoofing

Contractors upload arbitrary notebook pages, receipts, or stock photos to claim compliance points without verification.

03

Fragmented Salvage Chain

Demolition sites have excess salvageable lots but zero digital connection with aggregate buyers within delivery radius.

04

Missing ESG Telemetry

Zero automated computation of embodied carbon ($CO_2e$) offsets or municipal landfill airspace conserved.

Section 4

Assumptions and Risks

Category Key Assumption Identified Risk / Vulnerability Mitigation Strategy
Data Quality & Lighting Site photos captured via smartphone cameras in daylight or work-lights. Extreme dust, motion blur, and direct specular sun glares on metal scrap. Adaptive CLAHE contrast enhancement, multi-scale cropping, and color space normalization.
Material Contamination Debris piles may contain mixed composites (e.g., concrete slab with embedded rebar). Misclassification or ambiguous confidence scores on composite waste. Multi-label prediction ranking with human-in-the-loop override logging.
Network Connectivity Demolition sites have intermittent 4G/5G mobile bandwidth. Latency in heavy model inference or map tile loading. Client-side canvas tensor preprocessing and local disk-backed persistence caching.
Regulatory Integrity Contractors comply with municipal C&D waste disposal bylaws. Attempted submission of fraudulent paper receipts or non-debris images. Anti-spoofing document rejection algorithms, mandatory satellite GPS lock, and audit logging.

Section 9

Core Algorithms & Mathematical Models

1. Anti-Spoofing Document Filter

Integrity

Rejects non-debris certificates, notebooks, and invoices based on luminosity & stroke density:

Paper_Ratio = Σ[L_i > 165 ∧ S_i < 30] / N_pixels
Stroke_Ratio = Σ[L_i < 120] / N_pixels
REJECT IF (Paper > 0.65 ∧ 0.003 ≤ Stroke ≤ 0.09 ∧ σ < 40)

2. ResNet-34 Feature Classifier

Deep Learning

Residual skip connections overcoming vanishing gradients across 34 convolutional layers:

y = F(x, {W_i}) + x   (Identity Skip)
Head: AdaptiveAvgPool → Linear(512, 256) → Softmax
P(Class_k) = exp(z_k) / Σ exp(z_j)

3. Haversine Logistics Distance

Geospatial

Computes true great-circle spherical distance between demolition site and buyer aggregate yard:

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ1)·cos(φ2)·sin²(Δλ/2)
d = 2R · atan2(√a, √(1-a))   [R = 6,371 km]

4. Embodied Carbon & ESG Metrics

LCA Formula

Continuous mathematical conversion of diverted kilogram tonnage into carbon credits:

CO2e_Avoided (kg) = Diverted (kg) × 0.428 kg CO2e/kg
Landfill_Volume (m³) = Diverted (kg) / 1,250 kg/m³
Tipping_Saved (₹) = (Diverted / 1,000) × ₹1,800/ton

Haversine Distance marketplace.service.ts 
const a = Math.sin(dLat/2)**2 + 
  Math.cos(lat1) * Math.cos(lat2) * 
  Math.sin(dLon/2)**2;
const d = 2 * 6371 * 
  Math.atan2(Math.sqrt(a), Math.sqrt(1-a));
return +d.toFixed(1); // km
